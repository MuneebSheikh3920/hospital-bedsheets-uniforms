Do you sell in retail?

No, we supply on a wholesale basis only, for hospitals, clinics and retailers.

Which quality is best for a hospital?

Poly cotton blends (70/30 or 50/50) are the most common because they survive repeated hot washing. Pure cotton and percale suit private rooms.
